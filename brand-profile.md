# Brand Profile

Fill this in once. It's the single most important thing for getting content that
sounds like you and points at your offer. Replace the _italic prompts_ with your
answers — a sentence or two each is plenty. Delete any line that doesn't apply.

---

## 1. Who you are
_One line: your name/brand and what you help people do._
> Example: I'm a fitness coach who helps busy dads get strong without living in the gym.

## 2. Your niche
_The space you play in, specifically. Narrow is better than broad._
> Example: Strength & habit coaching for men 35–50 who travel for work.

## 3. Ideal customer
_Who you're talking to. Their situation, what they want, what's in their way, and the
objection they make before buying._
- Who they are: _e.g., mid-career dads, time-poor, used to be athletic_
- What they want: _e.g., feel strong and energetic again, set an example for their kids_
- What's stopping them: _e.g., no time, past programs felt all-or-nothing_
- Their #1 objection: _e.g., "I can't commit to an hour a day"_

## 4. Your offers
_What you sell and roughly the price points. Mark the ONE you most want followers to
move toward._
> Example: 12-week 1:1 coaching ($1,500) [primary] · self-paced program ($199) · free habit guide

## 5. Primary Instagram goal
_Pick one: awareness / leads / sales / authority / community. What does a "win" look like?_
> Example: Leads — DMs from dads who want the free guide, which feeds my coaching pipeline.

## 6. Brand voice
_3–5 words for how you sound, plus anything to avoid._
> Example: Direct, encouraging, no-BS, a little funny. Avoid: hype, shame, jargon.

## 7. Content pillars
_3–5 themes you post about. Everything you make should ladder up to one of these._
> Example: (1) quick strength workouts, (2) habit/mindset, (3) myth-busting, (4) client wins

## 8. Your point of view
_What you believe that others in your niche don't — your hot takes and hills you die on._
> Example: You don't need more discipline, you need a smaller plan. Soreness ≠ progress.

## 9. Call to action
_Where you send people and the exact action you want. Be specific._
> Example: "DM me STRONG for the free guide." / Link in bio to book a call.

## 10. Constraints & preferences
_What you can realistically make and sustain, and anything off-limits._
- Formats I can make: _e.g., talking-head Reels, carousels — no fancy editing_
- Cadence I can sustain: _e.g., 3 posts/week_
- Topics to avoid: _e.g., politics, extreme diets_

## 11. Swipe file (optional)
_Posts of yours that worked, and 2–3 accounts whose content you admire._
> Example: My "3-minute hotel room workout" Reel. Admire: @account1, @account2.
